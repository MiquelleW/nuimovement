Booking Information

Deposits and Rescheduling

A deposit is required to secure all N.U.i. Media Services bookings. Deposits are non-refundable if a client cancels their booking. Rescheduling is allowed based on availability.

Travel

Standard pricing covers Charlotte, NC and surrounding areas. Additional travel fees may apply for locations outside of our standard service area.

Large Events

Additional shooters may be required for larger events based on guest count, venue size, event schedule, and coverage needs. Additional shooter fees will be included in your custom quote.
